Questions

Questions you might have.

Something else? Email our client support team.

clientsupport@troopers.com.my
Do I pay to sign up?
No. Creating an account is free.
What do I need to get started?
Your SSM documents (your company registration) and some credits in your account. You can top up by card or business online banking. Then you can post your first job.
How long until my job goes up?
Our team checks every job within 3 working days. Once it is approved, you can chat with part-timers and hire them whenever you like.
How much does it cost?
You set the pay rate for each job. A service fee and SST are added on top. Late-night, public holiday and last-minute shifts cost extra. Open the rates in the Costs section above to see everything and work out your total.
What if I cancel a shift?
Cancelling close to the shift date has a charge. Open the rates in the Costs section above to see the details.
Can I ask for certificates?
Yes. When you post a job, you can ask for a Food Handler Certificate and a typhoid vaccination.
Which roles can I hire?
Service crew, kitchen crew, stewarding crew, baristas, banquet crew, hostesses, concierge, front desk, housekeeping and central kitchen crew. You can try them all in the "Part-timers near you" box above.
Where are the part-timers?
Part-timers have signed up in every Malaysian state, and in Singapore. The "Part-timers near you" section above shows the numbers.
