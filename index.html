03 · Workforce comparison

Three workforce models, very different trade-offs.

Local Full-Time Hiring

Employment Act 1955
  • Fixed hours, 1–3 month notice to terminate
  • EPF, SOCSO, annual leave: fixed monthly cost
  • Best for core, permanent operations

Foreign Workers

Levy, permit & FWCS
  • 4–6 week lead time for permit approval
  • Highest total cost of the three models
  • Best for stable, long-duration tasks

05 · How it works

Six steps from sign-up to hired.

Sign up once, then post a job whenever you need crew.

1

Create a free account

Sign up as an employer in the TROOPERS app. It is free.

2

Upload your SSM documents

Add your company registration papers (SSM) to your account.

3

Top up your credits

Add credits by card or business online banking before you post a job.

4

Post your job

Choose the role, how many people you need and your pay rate. Our team checks the job within 3 working days.

5

Chat and hire

When the job is approved, chat with the part-timers who apply and hire them whenever you like.

6

Pay after each shift

Credits are taken off automatically after the shift ends. You only pay for shifts that are completed.

13 · Questions

Questions you might have.

Do I pay to sign up?
No. Creating an account is free.
What do I need to get started?
Your SSM documents (your company registration) and some credits in your account. You can top up by card or business online banking. Then you can post your first job.
How long until my job goes up?
Our team checks every job within 3 working days. Once it is approved, you can chat with part-timers and hire them whenever you like.
How much does it cost?
You set the pay rate for each job. A service fee and SST are added on top. Late-night, public holiday and last-minute shifts cost extra. Open the rates in the Transparent pricing section above to see everything and work out your total.
What if I cancel a shift?
Cancelling close to the shift date has a charge. Open the rates in the Transparent pricing section above to see the details.
Can I ask for certificates?
Yes. When you post a job, you can ask for a Food Handler Certificate and a typhoid vaccination.
Which roles can I hire?
Service crew, kitchen crew, stewarding crew, baristas, banquet crew, hostesses, concierge, front desk, housekeeping and central kitchen crew. The full list is in the Roles section above.
Where are the part-timers?
Part-timers have signed up in every Malaysian state, and in Singapore. The map above shows the numbers.
